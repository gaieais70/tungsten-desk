# Tungsten Market Quarterly Intelligence Report (2025 – Q4 2026)

*Published: October 25, 2026*  
*Prepared for: Tungsten Desk*

---

## Executive Summary

Over the past seven quarters (Q1 2025 through October 2026), the global tungsten market experienced the most severe structural disruption in over fifty years. The catalyst was China's implementation of dual-use export controls on **February 4, 2025**, which broke the Rotterdam↔Shanghai physical arbitrage and created a bifurcated global market.

By July 2026, European Ammonium Paratungstate (APT 88.5% WO₃ CIF Rotterdam) reached an all-time record high of **$3,139.50 /mtu**, up nearly 10x from the opening 2025 baseline of **$330 /mtu**. In Autumn 2026, European prices stabilized in the **$2,500–$2,900 /mtu** corridor, while domestic Chinese APT settled near **RMB 480,000–500,000 /t (~$814 /mtu)**, maintaining a staggering **~178% export fragmentation premium**.

On **October 20, 2026**, CME Group announced the impending launch of the **first-ever exchange-traded Tungsten Futures contract**, cash-settled against the Fastmarkets APT Rotterdam/Baltimore assessment—a landmark development for commercial price discovery.

## 1. Quarter-by-Quarter Breakdown

### Q1 2025: The Export-Control Shock
- **Policy Landmark (Feb 4, 2025)**: China's Ministry of Commerce (MOFCOM) and General Administration of Customs placed tungsten, antimony, and related superhard materials under strict dual-use export controls. Exporters were required to submit end-user certificates, and dual-use defense applications were barred.
- **Price Action**: European APT opened 2025 at ~$330 /mtu. Immediate panic in Western supply chains pushed spot offers through $450 /mtu by late February.
- **Supply Constraints**: China's Ministry of Natural Resources (MNR) maintained tight first-half tungsten mining quotas, prioritizing state-owned smelting groups (Xiamen Tungsten, China Tungsten High-Tech).

### Q2 2025: Market Bifurcation Begins
- **Divergence**: Domestic Chinese APT held near RMB 165,000–175,000 /t (~$280 /mtu equivalent), while European APT reached $410–$450 /mtu.
- **Western Consumer Reaction**: Cutting tool manufacturers (Sandvik, Kennametal) and aerospace suppliers began auditing their supply chains for Chinese tungsten dependencies.
- **Downstream Hoarding**: European traders reported delays of 6–10 weeks for Chinese export license approvals.

### Q3 2025: Corporate Milestones & Upstream Tightening
- **Almonty NASDAQ Listing (July 2025)**: Almonty Industries completed its US redomicile and listing on the NASDAQ under the ticker `ALM`, positioning its Sangdong mine in South Korea as the premier non-China supply source.
- **Domestic Chinese Surge**: In August 2025, Chinese domestic APT breached the RMB 300,000 /t mark for the first time in modern history as domestic scrap prices soared.
- **European Benchmarks**: European APT reached $485–$520 /mtu in August and tested $650 /mtu by late September.

### Q4 2025: Regulatory Deadlines Loom
- **US Defense Procurement Ban**: The Pentagon reiterated compliance deadlines under **10 U.S.C. 4872 / DFARS 225.7018**, which prohibits the Department of Defense from acquiring tungsten melted or produced in China, Russia, or North Korea starting **January 1, 2027**.
- **Price Escalation**: European APT ended 2025 in the $735–$780 /mtu range.
- **US DLA Actions**: The Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) issued requests for information to restock the National Defense Stockpile with non-Chinese APT, ferro-tungsten, and tungsten carbide powder.

### Q1 2026: The Vertical Price Explosion
- **February 17, 2026 Explosion**: European APT exploded past $1,700 /mtu, reaching **$1,737 /mtu** on severe spot shortages. Spot availability outside China was virtually exhausted.
- **Downstream Resistance**: Heavy machinery and automotive manufacturers began signaling margin pressure as cemented carbide insert costs doubled.

### Q2 2026: Peak Volatility & Flash Correction
- **Late March Run-up**: Prices pushed past $2,600 /mtu in late March 2026.
- **April Correction**: Spot prices pulled back by >16% over 4 weeks into the $2,280–$2,420 /mtu range as European buyers temporarily refused higher offers and drew down inventories.
- **May–June Recovery**: By mid-June, European spot surged back to $2,700–$2,900 /mtu as scrap feedstock depleted and Chinese export license friction tightened further.

### Q3 2026: Historic All-Time Highs
- **July 24, 2026 All-Time High**: Fastmarkets assessed APT 88.5% CIF Rotterdam duty-free at a record **$3,139.50 /mtu**.
- **Domestic vs. Export Decoupling**: SMM and CTIA reported Chinese domestic APT transactions hovering around RMB 595,000–600,000 /t (~$1,005 /mtu), while Rotterdam held near $3,000 /mtu—a physical spread exceeding $1,900 /mtu.
- **IMARC Tungsten Carbide Print**: Tungsten carbide powder hit $131 /kg in China.

### Q4 2026 (September 15 – October 25, 2026): Stabilization & CME Futures
- **October Domestic Correction & Rebound**: Chinese domestic concentrate plunged ~20% in early October, testing the RMB 300,000 /standard ton mark. Major Chinese producers responded with centralized purchasing and firm floor guidance, triggering a **12% rebound** back above RMB 300,000 /t and stabilizing domestic APT near RMB 500,000 /t.
- **European Spot Softening**: European APT eased from $2,950 /mtu into the **$2,500–$2,900 /mtu** range (midpoint **$2,700 /mtu**).
- **CME Futures Announcement (Oct 20, 2026)**: CME Group officially announced the creation of cash-settled Tungsten Futures indexed against Fastmarkets' Rotterdam/Baltimore APT benchmark.
- **Vietnam Export Ban Proposal (Oct 16, 2026)**: Vietnam's Ministry of Industry and Trade announced draft regulations banning the export of unprocessed tungsten ores.

## 3. Policy & Regulatory Watch

1. **US DFARS 225.7018 Enforcement (January 1, 2027)**:
   - Full statutory ban on Department of Defense procurement of tungsten melted or produced in China, Russia, North Korea, or Iran.
   - Requires 100% auditable chain-of-custody tracking from mine to component.
2. **US Title III Defense Production Act (DPA) & Ex-Im Bank Financing**:
   - Multi-million-dollar funding awards earmarked for domestic US and allied extraction (Sangdong, Pilot Mountain, Mactung).
3. **European Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA)**:
   - Tungsten designated as both a Critical and Strategic Raw Material with targets for 10% domestic extraction, 40% domestic processing, and 25% recycling by 2030.
4. **China Total Mining Quotas**:
   - China's Ministry of Natural Resources (MNR) continues to limit primary mine production to protect remaining high-grade reserves in Jiangxi and Hunan.
5. **CME Futures Commercial Launch**:
   - Implementation of cash-settled contracts in late 2026 / early 2027 will create true forward-curve hedging for Western consumers for the first time.
