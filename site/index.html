Econometric Methodology: Pricing an Illiquid, Bifurcated Market

Tungsten lacks an active centralized exchange or transparent spot order book. Since 4 February 2025, when China's Ministry of Commerce (MOFCOM) and General Administration of Customs (GAC) instituted strict dual-use export licensing, Western assessments (CIF Rotterdam) and Chinese domestic pricing have decoupled. Fastmarkets characterises the structural phenomenon as "markets fragmenting".

Tungsten Desk synthesizes ten complementary econometric lenses (M0 through M9), culminating in the Final Rest-of-World (ROW) Assumed Tungsten Price ($1,717/mtu | $151,988/t APT) via bounded centered Ridge regressions, empirical metallurgical netback equations, hybrid logistics export parity, USD-normalized pure-play equity lead signals, and staleness-aware Bayesian Kalman filtering with RTS smoothing.

Model Assumptions & Risk Disclosures

  • Final ROW Assumed Composite Price (M6): Weighted benchmark ($1,717/mtu | $151,988/t) combining Ex-China Spot & Executable Clearing (30%), Econometric Lead & Value-Chain Nowcasts (44%), and Physical Netback, Incentive Cost Floor & Balance Anchors (26%) to represent a realistic clearing price for Rest-of-World commercial and project valuation. Cross-checked with Log-Geometric Mean ($1,715/mtu), Bates-Granger Precision ($2,191/mtu), and Trimmed Mean ($1,589/mtu).
  • Physical Assessment Illiquidity: CIF Rotterdam assessments reflect reported bilateral transactions and survey bids. With Chinese APT exports down 89.3%, Western transaction volumes are thin. The Rotterdam spot assessment ($2,700/mtu) reflects urgent Western unhedged restocking rather than long-term clearing value.
  • China Domestic Pricing Integrity: Domestic Chinese APT ($71,880/t = $812/mtu) and 65% Concentrate ($46,542/t = $714/mtu) are backed by 1,769 verified transaction records from CTIA, SMM, and Jiangxi smelters. Smelting parity matches domestic APT with level $R^2 = 0.9953$.
  • M2 Pure-Play Equities: The equity model extracts an implied spot price ($1,517/mtu) via centered constrained cointegrated log-return regression across all 12 pure-play companies in USD with Duan (1983) smearing ($R^2 = 0.918$). Equities lead physical APT by 21 trading days ($r = +0.602$), acting as an empirical early-warning indicator.
  • M4 Kalman State-Space Fusion: Fuses M0, M1, M2, and M3 with staleness-aware dynamic inverse-variance weighting and backward Rauch-Tung-Striebel (RTS) smoothing, yielding a Global Fundamental Fair Value of $1,471/mtu$ (90% band $1,223–$1,769/mtu) and a Western Executable Nowcast of $1,999/mtu$.
  • M5 Supply-Demand Equilibrium: Calibrated using Almonty Sangdong NI 43-101 deficit projections (5,570 t in 2025, 2,330 t in 2026), secondary price-elastic scrap elasticity, and 2.0% baseline demand CAGR. Inventory coverage is modeled since strategic reserves are not publicly disclosed.
  • M7–M9 Price Discovery Lenses: M7 captures the 90th-percentile marginal smelting AISC floor ($596/mtu) and Western DFARS greenfield incentive clearing price ($1,576/mtu); M8 nowcasts the 21-day econometric lead-lag impulse ($1,214/mtu); M9 computes ITIA downstream value-in-use netback parity ($1,232/mtu).
