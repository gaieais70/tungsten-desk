How we price an unpriced market

Tungsten has no exchange, no central clearing, and since 4 February 2025 — when China placed tungsten under its dual-use export-control regime — the two benchmark assessments (APT 88.5% WO₃ CIF Rotterdam and the Chinese domestic market) no longer form one market. Fastmarkets calls it "fragmenting". That fragmentation is our opportunity: instead of one "true" price, we publish six independent estimates, each with explicit assumptions, and let their divergence itself carry information. The Shanghai-implied + carry model (M1) is the cornerstone; the equity-implied model (M2) is a documented rejection; the supply-demand balance (M5) is the scenario forward-look.

Limitations — read before trading anything

  • The "reported" daily path between verified anchors is an analyst reconstruction, not a published assessment. Only the ISBP/Dornhofer anchors ($330 open-2025, >$3,000 on 17-Jul-2026), the Fastmarkets print ($3,139.50 on 24-Jul-2026), the IMARC carbide figure, and the export-control date are independently verified. The intermediate monthly shape is documented in M0 and is the largest source of model uncertainty.
  • The equity-implied model is rejected, not a price. It is kept solely as a documented diagnostic of why naive equity mapping fails (lead corr ≈ −0.19). It is excluded from the Kalman fusion.
  • The factor model is a sanity band, not a forecast. R² ≈ 0.42 — tungsten is policy- and demand-driven; copper is a control, iron ore and moly carry the demand signal.
  • The Shanghai-implied domestic price is reconstructed, not reported. We have no direct SMM feed; the domestic line is derived from the observed export price minus a documented fragmentation discount. Its level is the model's biggest single assumption.
  • The M5 supply-demand model rests on company-sourced balance figures (Sangdong NI 43-101 deficits, Almonty deck) and modeled, not reported, stock levels. Inventory in tungsten is invisible; policy shocks (export controls, the DoW 2027 procurement ban) can override any balance-driven price path.
  • This dashboard is a research prototype. Nothing here is investment advice.
